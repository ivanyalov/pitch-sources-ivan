## 4. How large is the opportunity, and why now?

- Defined segment, geography, unit of analysis, and time horizon: [ ]

- Reachable portion and constraints on access/distribution: [ ]

- Proposed headline: [ ]

Choose a model appropriate to the offering. For annual hardware revenue, a possible model is annual purchasing customers x units per purchasing customer x net unit price. For annual subscription revenue, a possible model is average paying accounts during the year x monthly price x 12. State the period and avoid counting the entire installed base as a fresh annual purchase. These are calculation aids, not forecasts or proof of demand.
